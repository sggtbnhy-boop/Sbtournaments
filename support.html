❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join a tournament?
Open the tournament list, choose an available match, and complete the registration process.
Where can I see my matches?
Open the Match History page to see your available match information and results.
What if my result is incorrect?
Contact the tournament administrator and provide the relevant match information for review.
What if I cannot join a match?
Check the match time and instructions first. If the problem continues, contact tournament support.

⚠️ Important

Never share your password, verification code, or other private account information with anyone. Only use the official tournament support method.
