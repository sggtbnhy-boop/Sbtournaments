📜 SB Tournament

Rules & Help Center

📋 General Rules

1 Players must provide correct information during registration.
2 Each player should use their registered account for the tournament.
3 Players must join the match before the announced start time.
4 Players should follow the instructions provided by the tournament admin.
5 Any tournament-specific rule shown on the match page must also be followed.

🎮 Match Rules

1 Use only the game account registered for the match.
2 Do not intentionally disrupt other players or the match.
3 Players should follow the announced room and match instructions.
4 Match results will be reviewed according to the tournament system.

🚫 Fair Play Rules

1 No cheating, hacks, exploits, or unauthorized game modifications.
2 No impersonating another player or submitting false information.
3 Suspicious results may be reviewed by the tournament administration.
4 Confirmed rule violations may result in match cancellation or account restriction.

🏆 Prize & Result Rules

Tournament prizes, if offered, will follow the specific prize information displayed on the relevant tournament page. Results should be checked after the match is completed.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How can I join a tournament?
Open the tournament list, select an available match, and complete the registration process.
Where can I see my match results?
Your available match information can be shown in the Match History section.
What should I do if there is a problem?
Contact the tournament administrator using the official support method provided by the website.
Can tournament rules change?
Yes. Tournament-specific rules may be updated by the administrator when necessary.
